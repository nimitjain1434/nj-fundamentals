How NFS works

v2.0 — price-aware score

The default headline now adds up to 60 business points and 40 valuation points. Business pillar proportions remain sector-specific, scaled to 60. The price multiple is latest traded Upstox price / latest annual diluted EPS, with the exchange trade time and financial year shown. It is not TTM or forward P/E. The sector's P/E anchors apply; mining, energy and cement use 25 / 18 / 12 / 8 / 5× at metric scores 0 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100. Banks use 35 / 25 / 18 / 12 / 8× where no P/E anchors exist.

Missing weights stay unearned and are never redistributed. A missing business pillar or total coverage below 80% caps the provisional score at 69. Business strength is shown separately as an observed-input diagnostic, along with its covered weight. A high business-strength score can coexist with an expensive-price score. Latest quotes refresh in one batch; quote downloads expire for scoring after 30 minutes, with a maximum last-trade age of seven days. Missing/invalid prices or EPS prevent a price-aware headline score.

Bajaj Finance's confirmed June 2025 split/bonus factor is applied only to EPS years that still use the old share basis, with an implied-share consistency check. Other unresolved material EPS/PAT share-basis jumps are flagged rather than treated as poor earnings growth. M&M cash conversion uses matching standalone parent-company PAT and cash flow where available, so lending cash flows are not treated as automotive cash conversion.

Automatic Upstox screen — the default score

The screener downloads existing Upstox data automatically. Its available-data variant uses reported ROCE/ROE, revenue/EPS growth, quick ratio/equity funding, operating cash conversion/history and reported P/E/EV-to-EBITDA. Banks use ROA/ROE, PAT/EPS growth, net NPA/capital where available, NIM/CASA and reported P/B adjusted for ROE plus P/E. It does not call PBT operating profit, total liabilities debt, or a current ratio a historical median.

Automatic score = points earned on available inputs ÷ available weight × 100, then apply known risk caps. It requires ≥50% weight, four metrics and three pillars. Missing metrics are excluded from this partial calculation and coverage is displayed. These indicative scores are separate from the complete model below; governance, cycle normalization and specialist disclosures remain incomplete. Missing insurer VNB/solvency/embedded-value data can prevent an insurer score. Provider ratio effective dates/basis are not supplied; retrieval dates are shown without inventing price dates. Downloads older than seven days are not scored.

Complete analyst model — optional supplementary analysis

Five pillars, 20 business models, ten metrics per model. Each pillar splits its weight 60:40 between two metrics. Governance and exceptional risks apply score caps.

These are initial NJ analyst assumptions, not a backtested model. Thresholds are absolute scoring anchors, not peer averages or regulatory requirements. Compare scores within the same business model.

Calculation and interpretation

Each metric is interpolated linearly between values assigned 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100; scores clamp at the endpoints. Points = metric score × weight / 100. E.g. a 75 metric score at 15% weight earns 11.25 points. Add points, apply the lowest risk cap, then round once to one decimal.

85–100 exceptional; 70–84.9 strong; 55–69.9 mixed; 40–54.9 weak; below 40 high risk / weak. These describe fundamentals and valuation, not expected returns or buy/sell recommendations.

Data and risk rules

Publish only with ≥80% weight coverage, ≥50% of every pillar and every critical (*) metric. Missing weight earns no points and is never redistributed. Partial coverage is provisional. Source references, valid reporting dates and user review are mandatory. Financial inputs expire after 550 days, valuation fundamentals after 200 days, prices after 7 days.

A governance review needs evidence and a date within 100 days. Unknown risk caps the score at 69; material concerns at 59; default, adverse audit, insolvency, non-positive equity or a negative regulatory capital buffer at 39. Interest coverage below 1×, net NPA ≥4% or net debt / EBITDA ≥5× caps at 49. Lowest cap wins. These risk triggers are NJ assumptions.

Research basis

The framework draws on MSCI quality concepts, RBI banking soundness dimensions, life-insurer solvency disclosures, and CFA Institute reporting-quality analysis. Exact weights, anchors, coverage rules and caps are NJ's own design. Regulatory minima must be verified for each entity and date.
